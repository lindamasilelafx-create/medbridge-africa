Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions from prospective students.

Do I need IELTS?

Most Russian medical universities do not require IELTS for English-medium medicine programs.

How long is medicine in Russia?

General Medicine programs usually take 6 years to complete.

Can South African students apply?

Yes. MedBridge Africa assists students from South Africa and across Africa.
